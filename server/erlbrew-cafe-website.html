About Erlbrew

Brewed in
Kawit's spirit.

Kawit, Cavite gave the Philippines its independence. We carry that spirit into every cup — refusing to be ordinary, refusing to rush. Erlbrew opened in 2026 with one idea: that a great coffee shop should feel like a small gift to whoever walks in.

Our menu pulls from two traditions — the precision of Italian espresso and the meditative craft of Japanese matcha. The result is a menu that's worth lingering over.
